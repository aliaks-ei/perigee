## Evidence and reconstruction boundary

Authoritative research preceded selection/generation of surface art. Moravveji's spectroscopy/photometry and the VEGA/CHARA and AMBER studies were reviewed first; the 2022 intensity-interferometry study was subsequently checked as corroboration. Retrieved on 2026-10-01. The reviewed sources constrain a disc, integrated variability and wind diagnostics; they do **not** supply a resolved visible photospheric pattern or a measured full-surface map. Resolving a diameter or an extended spectral-line region is different from reconstructing surface pixels. Spectral resolving power is not angular image resolution.

The original [`generate-rigel-texture.py`](../scripts/hybrid-review/generate-rigel-texture.py) creates **synthetic mottling** from four smooth, continuous 3D spatial bands with a modest vector domain warp and differently oriented octave bases. This is an artistic way to retain the portrait's coherent blue-white variation, not a solar granulation or Betelgeuse convection model. There are no measured cell sizes, physical feature lifetimes, current patterns, spots, companions, orbits or wind structures. Pixel dimensions describe the artwork's sampling only; they assert no observational resolution. Neither portrait nor telescope pixels enter the generated assets. No baked disc, limb, halo, stars or background is wrapped onto the globe.

New assets, provenance and rights are recorded here and in `rigel-mottling-review-v1.json`; public credits stayed unchanged throughout review and now record the approved synthetic globe. The source script and textures are project-authored under MIT. The 4096×2048 lossless WebP uses equirectangular body coordinates; a 2048×1024 orthographic north/south atlas samples the identical field. The shader reconstructs longitude from normalized body position, supplies analytic UV gradients across the seam and blends to padded pole patches without erasing polar detail.

## Colour, optics and motion

The live order is HDR scene → bloom → AgX → display-linear portrait layer → production film/SMAA → final sRGB encoding. The fixed portrait is display-referred. `TextureCache` marks colour textures sRGB, with top-down decoding; texture sampling decodes once. The Rigel-only globe and its separate optical plane reuse this post-AgX layer with `toneMapped:false`. No manual second gamma/AgX or scene-exposure change is applied. This is authored display colour and self-emission, **not calibrated Rigel photometry**. `StillRenderer` applies AgX to the HDR scene, renders the same display layer into its linear target and performs final sRGB output. Alpha is straight, the photosphere is opaque at full visibility, and transition opacity applies to surface and optics together.

The photosphere uses observer-angle continuum/limb grading, without a directional light or terminator. A continuous highlight shoulder preserves bright structure. The separate view-facing optical plane never inherits surface rotation. Orthographic sphere projection onto the existing sky tangent plane keeps the off-axis centre and apparent radius aligned with the portrait and halo. Production post-processing stays fixed.

The initial native 8K inspection exposed excessively curled, sharp mottling and a white rim. One correction batch reduced the warp, softened the spectral mix/contrast, restored the blue luminous edge and narrowed optical spill. The final confirming inspection used complete saved frames, unscaled surface/limb/halo crops and native quarter/pole views. It found no critical visual defect in those conditions. Remaining artistic differences are documented below.

Projected speed is not a measured period: v sin i needs a radius and inclination to infer equatorial rotation. Moravveji's inclination range and tentative line-phase sky orientation are model-dependent; wind signals are distinct from photospheric rotation. Direction/sign, a defensible pole registration, reference epoch and a complete reference-frame convention remain unreviewed. Therefore `objectMotion.rigel` keeps null period, disabled policy and unknown confidence. The legacy `rotationPeriodHours:1200` was preserved during review but never consumed as a motion model. Approval now permits its removal from public metadata; no replacement period or motion control is added.

Placement → fixed pole → spin → surface follows the accepted adapter. Longitude is a deterministic **review turntable**, with null period; the authored placement tilt is not an astronomical pole. No public rotation control or illustrative evolution was added. The mapped surface stays fixed as the clock advances. `CelestialClock`, reduced motion, pause/visibility suspension, cancellation and idempotent texture leasing/disposal are reused. Every tile/sample in a still uses one immutable pose; capture completion releases the clock without hidden-time catch-up.
