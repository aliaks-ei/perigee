The initial map's straight angular lanes were refined with continuous domain warping, fuller seed jitter, larger illustrative cells and brighter interiors. Native export inspection then exposed overly smooth, regular cells and a peach cast. The selected recipe adds multiscale body-space mottling, variable lane widths, stronger domain warp, 0.4-pixel periodic smoothing and a more golden grade; analytical penumbrae have irregular boundaries and finer broken fibers. The shader's centre-continuum correction restores warm ivory with a continuous 0.94-to-0.995 linear highlight shoulder; the optical spill was narrowed. These are artistic adjustments to the fixed reference, not new measurements. Monochromatic telescope data do not establish that colour. Spot sizes and positions are invented, with restrained umbra/penumbra structure and weak near-limb facular contrast.

## Colour and projection decision

The texture cache marks colour maps sRGB and uses `flipY=false`, so sampler output is decoded to linear colour. The generated map's bottom row is north and top row south, matching Three SphereGeometry UV; longitude wraps and the field is sampled in continuous 3D coordinates at both poles. Exact-pole inspection exposed a radial pinching artifact from equirectangular sampling. Body-space spherical coordinates and explicit seam-safe gradients alone did not eliminate the polar filtering singularity. The selected shader blends to padded orthographic north/south patches of the **same field** between local |Y| 0.90 and 0.97. Their UVs and explicit gradients have no longitude singularity. This preserves cap detail without stretching, replacing it with a blank cap or inventing another surface. No extra manual gamma decode is applied.

The live composer order is HDR sky → bloom → vignette/AgX → `PortraitPass` → production film → SMAA. The Sun portrait uses `toneMapped: false` after AgX. The review emissive sphere and separate optical spill use the same display-linear layer, also with `toneMapped: false`. This is a consciously **display-referred** stellar art path, not radiometrically calibrated HDR solar emission. It preserves the reference hue and exposure, avoids double AgX and bloom flattening, and does not alter another renderer. The existing unresolved point stays in its established HDR/bloom path.

`StillRenderer` already detects `displayReferredGlobe`, uses a half-float linear display target, applies AgX explicitly to the HDR scene, composites the globe, then encodes sRGB once. No shared live/export colour path was changed. Live film grain is zero with reduced motion, while separate normal-motion browser checks exercise production film. Exposure, viewpoint, sky, distance, post-processing and calculated radius are held constant for comparisons.

A perspective sphere at the off-axis sky position displaced its silhouette relative to the portrait's tangent-plane optical layer. The corrected sphere vertex shader projects its rotating three-dimensional surface orthographically onto that **same sky tangent plane**. Its sphere geometry, UVs, normals and complete-surface turntable remain genuine; the projected disc has the portrait's calculated diameter and centre. There is no hidden change to the physical radius or camera framing. The shader uses observer-angle limb behaviour, no Sun-light terminator, a continuous highlight shoulder and a clean luminous edge. The optical spill is view-facing and never rotates with the map; it is an authored optical effect, not a measured corona.

## Motion audit and controls

`objectMotion.sun` is intentionally unchanged: the existing differential policy tag is not a rotation solution, its period is null and its confidence remains unknown. The promoted globe has a fixed spin frame; only explicit review turntables/differential demos move it. No public period or rotation control was introduced.

`solarRotation.ts` implements the rounded original Snodgrass–Ulrich abstract coefficients, in **sidereal degrees per day**, using latitude φ measured from the equator. North is local +Y. Positive/right-handed prograde motion is counterclockwise when viewed from north; longitude is `atan(-z,x)`. The rate is symmetric north/south and falls from 14.71 deg/day at the equator (24.473-day period) to 10.54 deg/day at the pole (34.156 days). This describes the paper's Doppler-feature pattern, not every solar structure.

[SunPy's frame documentation](https://docs.sunpy.org/en/stable/generated/api/sunpy.sun.models.differential_rotation.html) distinguishes sidereal from apparent Earth-observed/synodic motion: Earth's orbital motion makes the latter slower. Subtracting an average ~0.986 deg/day would give approximately 26.23 days at the equator; we **do not apply** that approximation to this fixed hypothetical observer or claim an Earth-observed ephemeris. Do not confuse this fit with a Carrington frame or SunPy's differently sourced `snodgrass` 1983 option.

Placement root → fixed pole → spin → surface follows the approved lifecycle. The root retains the existing authored pitch/yaw/roll; the north direction is a presentation convention, not a dated solar pole/P-angle/B0 solution. Complete-surface turntables use `longitudeDegrees` and null-period spin. The review-only `globe-differential` demo moves analytical feature **centres**, preserving each spot's shape; static granulation is never sheared. It stops at eight days with no cyclic reset. Independently bounded illustrative spot-size evolution does not change longitude. Neither mechanism simulates real emergent/decaying active regions or their lifetimes.

Review URLs (port 4318, outside Nuxt pages):

- `index.html?object=sun&renderer=globe&longitude=0` — frozen reference. Use 90/180/270; `pole=north` / `pole=south`; `quality=balanced` / `safe`; any existing Sun `distance` preset.
- `index.html?object=sun&renderer=globe&solarDays=4&solarEvolutionDays=0` — deterministic four-day advection epoch without spot-size evolution.
- `index.html?object=sun&renderer=differential&solarDays=0&solarEvolutionDays=0` — opt-in clock demonstration, stops at eight days. Independent `CelestialClock` spin/evolution snapshots drive it; pause, reduced motion and hidden-tab suspension apply.
- `renderer=portrait` / `renderer=production` — the unchanged fixed reference / active product policy.

Texture acquisition, cancellation, idempotent leases, quality selection and the resolved-to-point transition reuse the existing factory and object adapter. Still captures freeze a single clock snapshot for all tiles/samples, including the bounded analytical spot positions and evolution. No new animation timer or motion system was introduced.
