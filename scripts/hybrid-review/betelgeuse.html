Sources and remaining differences

The 1774×887 global convection map is generated artwork, not measured Betelgeuse surface data. ALMA’s 2023 observations and ESO’s optical images support large, changing asymmetries, but they do not supply a registered visible-light map of the far side. The map’s exact cells, colours, meridian and pole treatment are artistic. The seam is blended; its source contains synthetic polar structure.

The globe has different individual bright regions and finer texture than the commissioned portrait. The portrait’s bloom and tonal character are an illustration, so exact morphology cannot be recovered from observations. No stellar rotation period or pole is asserted; quarter turns are deterministic inspection poses only. The 95% rubric weighs detail 30%, lighting 25%, colour 20%, silhouette 15% and composition 10%, separately on both layouts. Tests and pixel diagnostics do not grant visual approval. Physical-phone behavior is untested.
