Observations, reconstruction and limits

Color comes from ESA/DLR/FU Berlin’s HRSC high-altitude RGB bands (2 km/pixel), combined with NASA MGS/TES regional brightness and the older NASA Viking merged-color mosaic (23,040 × 11,520). Actual native Viking detail feeds a bounded 2K/4K/8K/16K pyramid. HRSC coverage gaps contain upstream interpolation; their exact fraction is not supplied. TES, HRSC and Viking are different epochs, so this is a display reconstruction, not a new calibrated albedo retrieval.

The existing Viking MDIM 2.1 supplies polar color, blended at 68–78° latitude; no cap was painted. MOLA supplies unchanged physical terrain with attenuated normal response. Old photographed shadows, gridding, polar interpolation and source-resolution limits remain. The new 11 km-scale-height atmosphere approximates single scattering. Procedural and MOC/Hubble-derived cloud-mask experiments were rejected; extraction introduced surface contamination or distracting bands. No added cloud layer is enabled. Geographic surface features are observational.

The sidereal period is 24.6229 hours (not the solar day). At 120× a turn takes 738.687 seconds. Local +Y is north, prograde is right-handed, the pole and world-space Sun stay fixed. Shot orientation is authored, not an ephemeris. Reduced motion, hidden-tab suspension and frozen exports reuse the approved infrastructure. There are no public rotation settings.
