Sources and remaining differences

NASA’s CGI Moon Kit supplies the LROC color and LOLA elevation. Color is photometrically normalized and adjusted for display. The polar caps use lower-resolution laser reflectance and upstream infill; terrain grids include interpolation. Faint source mosaic/reflectance markings remain visible in some far-side views. No craters or rays are generated or repeated.

Review v5 restores small ray and crater contrast using only the observed reflectance, with bounded enhancement and more diffuse lighting. The globe preserves near-side geography, neutral regolith and a fixed light direction. Individual crater shapes, ray prominence and local tonal patterns differ from the commissioned portrait. Its colors and lighting are authored display approximations, not calibrated photometry. The Moon has no rendered atmospheric glow.

The Moon rotates synchronously with its orbit, keeping substantially the same hemisphere toward Earth. This fixed observer model holds that face. The review quarter turns inspect the map; they are not a public free-spin or libration simulation.

The approval rubric is detail 30%, lighting 25%, color 20%, silhouette 15% and composition 10%, independently for both layouts. No numerical score or visual acceptance is inferred from tests. Physical-phone, thermal, touch and other-browser behavior remain untested.
