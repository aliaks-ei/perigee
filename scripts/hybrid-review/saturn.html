Sources and reconstruction limits

The 3600 × 1801 surface combines Cassini 2011 cloud structure, Hubble OPAL 2024 broad haze and a Cassini 2013 polar view. It is an authored multi-epoch reconstruction, not simultaneous weather or calibrated albedo. Missing strips and the longitude seam are interpolated. The rings combine Cassini 2008 display color and independent Voyager 1981 optical depth. Pole, light, palette and longitude are authored shot settings. Weak perspective preserves the portrait framing; the globe is oblate and the rings remain equatorial. Approximate ringshine and bounded shadow fill preserve readable indirect light.

The clock uses the Cassini ring-seismology estimate of 10h 33m 38s. The review's disclosed 120× time-lapse makes one turn in 316.8 seconds. Rotation settings are absent from the product; accepted objects spin automatically, respecting reduced motion.
